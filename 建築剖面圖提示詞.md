# School Empire 建築剖面圖（透視用）提示詞

## 這是什麼
按「透視」時，建築的外牆會消失，露出裡面的房間，小人在裡面走動。
我們需要 **8 種建築** 的室內剖面圖。小人由遊戲自己畫上去，所以**圖裡不可以有任何人**。

需要剖面的建築：教室樓、實驗室、圖書館、宿舍、餐廳、醫護室、實習工廠、研究中心。
（其他建築如操場、游泳池本來就看得到人，不需要。）

## 建議順序
1. 先做 **2005–2035 年（現代）** 這一批 8 張，我放進遊戲確認效果。
2. 效果滿意，再做其他 4 個年代，每個年代 8 張，共 32 張。
3. 沒有專屬年代圖時，遊戲會用現代這一批補上。

## 做法（很重要）
- **每棟建築一張，分開生成**，不要放同一張總覽。
- **把同一棟的外觀圖附上去當參考**（就是你之前給我的那張），這樣大小、形狀、風格才會對得上。
- 底色一樣用純洋紅 #FF00FF。

## 通用提示詞（每張都貼這一段，再接上下面該建築的描述）

```
Isometric cutaway interior of a school building, same 2:1 isometric camera angle as the attached reference image, hand-painted Studio Ghibli watercolor style, warm soft light, clean outlines.

The building footprint is a diamond (rhombus) shape on the ground, identical in size and orientation to the reference exterior. The two FRONT walls (facing the viewer, bottom-left and bottom-right) are completely removed so we can see inside. Only the two BACK walls (top-left and top-right) remain, about one story tall, with windows, doors and wall decorations. The floor is a flat visible diamond slab with a thin edge showing its thickness. NO roof.

The room is completely EMPTY OF PEOPLE: no students, no teachers, no characters at all. Leave open walkable floor space in the middle and along the edges so characters can be drawn walking on top later. Furniture stays close to the back walls and corners.

Background: solid flat pure magenta #FF00FF only, no shadow on the background, no gradient, no ground outside the building, no trees outside the floor slab. The whole picture must fit inside the frame with a margin around it. No text, no watermark, no UI.
```

## 各建築的描述（接在通用提示詞後面）

### 1. 教室樓 classroom（佔地 3×2）
```
A classroom: rows of student desks and chairs along the back walls, a blackboard on the back-left wall, a teacher's desk in the corner, bookshelf, wall posters, a potted plant on the window sill. Leave a wide empty aisle in the middle.
```

### 2. 實驗室 lab（佔地 2×2）
```
A science laboratory: long lab benches with beakers and flasks, a microscope, a sink, a cabinet of chemicals on the back-left wall, a periodic table poster on the back-right wall, safety shower in the corner. Leave an empty aisle between the benches.
```

### 3. 圖書館 library（佔地 3×2）
```
A library: tall bookshelves full of books along both back walls, a few reading tables with lamps near the walls, a checkout counter in the corner, a ladder, a globe. Leave a wide empty aisle in the middle.
```

### 4. 宿舍 dorm（佔地 3×2）
```
A dormitory room: bunk beds and single beds with blankets along the back walls, small desks, wardrobes, a shelf, laundry hanging in one corner, a rug. Leave the middle floor empty.
```

### 5. 餐廳 canteen（佔地 3×2）
```
A school canteen: a food serving counter with trays and pots along the back-left wall, long dining tables with benches near the back-right wall, a few round tables in the corner, hanging lamps, a menu board. Leave the middle floor empty for walking.
```

### 6. 醫護室 clinic（佔地 2×1，比較小）
```
A school infirmary: two small clinic beds with white sheets, a medicine cabinet with bottles, a desk with a stethoscope, a weighing scale, a red cross sign on the wall, a small sink. Small compact room. Leave the middle floor empty.
```

### 7. 實習工廠 workshop（佔地 3×2）
```
A school workshop: lathe and drill machines, workbenches with tools, a tool wall with hanging tools, wooden crates, a few barrels, oil lamps or ceiling lights. Leave a wide empty aisle in the middle.
```

### 8. 研究中心 research（佔地 3×2）
```
A research center: computer desks with monitors, whiteboards with diagrams, a server rack, a small telescope by the window, a model globe or atom model, stacks of papers. Leave the middle floor empty.
```

## 年代修飾（需要其他年代時，加在最後）

| 年代 | 加這一句 |
|---|---|
| 1930 年前 | `Early 1900s style: wooden floor, wooden desks, kerosene lamps, paper windows, no electric appliances.` |
| 1930–1975 | `1930s to 1970s style: wooden desks, blackboards, ceiling fans, plain plaster walls, simple bulbs.` |
| 1975–2005 | `1980s to 1990s style: tiled floor, steel desks, fluorescent ceiling lights, CRT computers, wall-mounted fans.` |
| 2005–2035 | `Modern style: clean floor, projector, flat screens, air conditioners, LED lights.` |
| 2035 年後 | `Near-future style: glass partitions, holographic screens, sleek furniture, plants on the walls, soft glowing lights.` |

## 檢查清單（生成後先看這幾點再丟給我）
- 有沒有人？有的話請重做。
- 前面兩面牆是不是真的拿掉了？
- 地板是不是完整的菱形？
- 有沒有屋頂？不要屋頂。
- 底色是不是純洋紅？

## 我這邊怎麼接
我會把剖面圖切下來，對齊到外觀同一個位置，按透視時換成剖面圖，小人直接畫在上面。
先做 1–2 棟給我試，我調好位置和大小再請你做剩下的，這樣不會白做。
